AI-003 / Community Artwork

AI Artwork Submission

Reimagine the Quantum Hamster with AI — the best entries earn $QHAM.

How it works

  1. Start from the template. Download the base hamster image and reimagine it however you like — new outfits, poses, styles, or backgrounds — using any AI image tool you have access to (Gemini, ChatGPT, Midjourney, or anything else).
  2. Submit your artwork. Post it on X with the hashtag #QHAMArtwork and tag @QHamsterAI, or drop it in the Discord artwork channel. Include the wallet address you'd like a reward sent to if you win.
  3. Get picked. The team reviews new submissions periodically and picks favorites based on creativity, quality, and how well it fits the Quantum Hamster vibe.
  4. Earn $QHAM. Winning entries receive a $QHAM reward, sent directly to the wallet address included with the submission.

Rewards are currently paid in testnet $QHAM on Base Sepolia as part of ongoing experimentation. Nothing here is financial advice, and reward mechanics may change as the project evolves.
